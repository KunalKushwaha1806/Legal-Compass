✦ About Legal Compass

Why Legal Compass?

We believe every Indian citizen deserves access to clear, affordable legal information — without complicated jargon or expensive consultations.

🤖

NLP-Powered Engine

Trained on Indian legal corpus — Constitution, IPC, CrPC — our AI delivers context-aware answers powered by TF-IDF retrieval and a fine-tuned Flan-T5 model.

⚡

Instant Responses

Get immediate answers to legal questions anytime, anywhere. Our query-response pipeline processes your question in milliseconds, backed by a Flask REST API.

🛡️

Secure & Persistent

All conversations are stored locally using SQLite. Your session history is private — no third-party data sharing, no tracking, no cloud dependency.

✦ Our Process

How It Works

Our NLP pipeline processes your legal query in three intelligent steps.

1

Ask Your Question

Type your legal question in plain English or Hindi. No jargon needed — describe your situation naturally.

2

NLP Analysis

Our TF-IDF retrieval engine searches the Indian legal corpus. If a fine-tuned Flan-T5 model is available, it generates a bespoke answer.

3

Instant Answer

Receive a clear, cited answer with the relevant legal provision, source, and category — stored securely in SQLite.
