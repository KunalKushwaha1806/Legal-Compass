# LegalCompass: AI-Powered Legal Assistant Chatbot  

LegalCompass is an AI-driven platform designed to provide accessible and accurate legal information to the general public. The chatbot simplifies legal concepts using advanced Natural Language Processing (NLP) to answer queries in an intuitive, user-friendly way.

---

## 📚 **Project Overview**

LegalCompass bridges the gap between complex legal terminology and the general public by democratizing access to legal knowledge. Its features empower users to understand basic legal concepts related to contracts, property, business, and criminal law without the need for expensive consultations.  

### **Key Features**
- **AI-Powered Legal Responses:** Provides accurate and context-aware answers using NLP models.
- **User-Friendly Website Design:** A responsive and futuristic interface accessible on multiple devices.
- **Cost-Effective Legal Information:** Offers free basic information and premium services for advanced queries.
- **Ad Integration for Revenue:** Features Google AdSense for sustainable monetization.

---

## 🎯 **Objectives**
- Simplify legal knowledge for the general public.
- Utilize AI and NLP to deliver accurate, real-time responses to legal queries.
- Provide a user-friendly interface that enhances accessibility.
- Offer affordable solutions for basic legal questions while maintaining legal compliance.

## 🛠️ **Technologies Used**
### **Backend & AI Development**
- **Python 3.9** for core development.
- **Flask 2.0** for web backend.
- **TensorFlow 2.0 / PyTorch** for AI and Machine Learning models.  

### **Frontend**
- **HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript** for responsive UI/UX.

### **Database**
- **MySQL / SQLite** for efficient data management.  

### **Additional Tools**
- **Google AdSense API** for ad integration.
- **Git** for version control.

## ⚙️ **System Requirements**

### **Hardware**
- **CPU:** Intel i5 or higher (Quad-Core, 2.5 GHz+).  
- **RAM:** 8 GB (16 GB recommended).  
- **GPU:** Integrated GPU or NVIDIA GTX 1050 for AI training.  
- **Storage:** 256 GB SSD (512 GB recommended).  

### **Software**
- **OS:** Windows 10 / Linux (Ubuntu 20.04) / macOS.
