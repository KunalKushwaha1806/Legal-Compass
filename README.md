# Legal-Compass
LegalCompass is an AI-powered platform designed to provide users with basic legal 
information. The project aims to bridge the gap between complex legal jargon and the general 
public's understanding by simplifying legal knowledge in an easily accessible format. The field 
of this project falls under LegalTech, which leverages technology to enhance the delivery of 
legal services and make legal information more transparent and widely available. The platform 
will feature automated AI responses to legal queries, making it a cost-effective and user-friendly 
tool for legal awareness.
The key technical terms associated with this project include AI-based knowledge processing, 
natural language processing (NLP), and legal information systems. These technologies help 
LegalCompass deliver accurate and contextually relevant legal information to users. By utilizing 
a futuristic and advanced website design, the project aims to create an interactive and intuitive 
user interface that enhances the user experience.
